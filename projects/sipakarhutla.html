SIPAKARHUTLA.

Android app for recording and reviewing forest and land fire assessments after a fire.

ROLEAndroid Developer
STACKKotlin · Android SDK · Room · Google Maps · TensorFlow Lite
PERIODJan—Aug 2026
CONTEXTThesis · Faculty project

Project overview

SIPAKARHUTLA supports field officers documenting post-fire conditions and plot observations. The Android app brings together location capture, map-based area recording, observation forms, and severity information.

My contribution

  • Continued an existing Kotlin Android app, with mobile development as my main focus.
  • Worked on offline location capture, saved assessment drafts, and draft recovery for field data entry.
  • Built map-based polygon workflows and contributed to report and plot editing features.
  • Integrated on-device image classification with TensorFlow Lite and supported REST API and backend updates.
