SILALA.

Flutter mobile prototype for BPMSPH’s digital library and archive service, designed to make books and technical references easier to explore.

ROLEMobile Application Developer Intern
STACKFlutter · Dart
PERIODSep—Dec 2025
DELIVERABLEMobile prototype

Project overview

Staff needed an easier way to find books, procedures, regulations, and technical references held in the library and archive. SILALA was planned to make collection details and digital reading materials easier to access.

My contribution

  • Designed and built the main Flutter screens, adapting the existing website design for phone use.
  • Implemented navigation and basic validation across login, registration, home, catalogue, and book-detail screens.
  • Built mobile flows for search, category browsing, favorites, borrowing and reading history, and PDF viewing.
  • Refined layouts and interactions while developing the prototype with sample data.
